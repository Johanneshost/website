07 / Product design

Yellow Superpress

A mechanical citrus press for professional bars. Instead of pressing down, it presses sideways, so lemons are squeezed with much less force: one-handed and without electricity.

Where
HTW Berlin, Design Basics 3
Supervision
Prof. Franziska Schuh · Dipl.-Des. Peggy Truxa · Prof. Jan Vietze
Semester
Summer 2025
Methods
Sketching · CAD (Rhino) · Rendering · Model making
Hochschule für Technik
und Wirtschaft Berlin

Concept

The glass stands directly beneath the press for clean, fast work, and a small inlet holds a second glass for seeds and pulp. Sketches explored containers, cones and stools; three CAD variants traced the form from a small table on legs to one continuous curved surface.
