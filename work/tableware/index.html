03 / User-centred product design

Tableware for Construction Workers

A robust lunch set designed around the day of a construction worker: durable, easy to carry and as tough-looking as the tools around it.

Where
San Francisco State University
Duration
4 weeks
Methods
Research · Sketching · CAD · Vacuum forming
Software
Fusion

Process

The project started from a persona: Bob, a construction worker whose lunch break happens on the tailgate of a pick-up. Sketches tested stacking, grip and size; the final shapes were modelled in CAD, milled as wooden moulds, vacuum-formed and spray-finished in safety yellow.
