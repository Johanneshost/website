08 / Design Project 2 · Charité

Masseo

An add-on for wireless in-ears that turns a bruxism guard into a lifestyle product.

Where
HTW Berlin, with Charité dental clinic
Supervision
Prof. Pelin Celik
Semester
Summer 2026
Methods
Research · Sketching · 3D modelling · Rendering
Hochschule für Technik
und Wirtschaft Berlin

Brief

A medical silicone pad on the cheek reads the masseter muscle via EMG and relaxes it with short bursts of heat and vibration. It draws power from the earbud through a flexible cable; at night a magnetic range-extender battery takes over, and an iOS app logs the clenching.
