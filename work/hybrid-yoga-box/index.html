04 / Product design

Hybrid Yoga Box

A dual-use yoga block that merges natural craftsmanship with technology.

Where
HTW Berlin
Duration
1 month
Materials
Wood · Cork · Electronics
Methods
CNC milling · Model making
Hochschule für Technik
und Wirtschaft Berlin

Concept

A precision CNC-milled wooden core wrapped in high-grip, sustainable cork houses an integrated Bluetooth speaker for immersive sound during practice. A companion box stores a water bottle, and a Velcro system snaps both units together for maximum stability.
